## Concepts covered
1. **Electric charge (மின்னூட்டம்)** — positive/negative charge, attraction/repulsion, coulomb.
2. **Electric current (மின்னோட்டம்)** — rate of flow of charge, ampere, closed circuit.
3. **Potential difference / voltage (மின்னழுத்த வேறுபாடு)** — work per unit charge, volt, voltmeter.
4. **Resistance (மின்தடை)** — opposition to current, ohm, dependence on material/length/area/temperature.
5. **Ohm's law (ஓம் விதி)** — V = I × R at constant temperature.
6. **Electric circuit (மின்சுற்று)** — closed path, cell, wires, switch, bulb.
